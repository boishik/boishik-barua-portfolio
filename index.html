R / 01
Research · Computer Vision · Generative AI2025 — Present

Identity-Aware Face Reconstruction using Hierarchical Geometry Conditioning

Developed a compact HGC face-reconstruction framework that combines facial landmarks, 12-class semantic parsing, Canny edges, and structured facial-attribute text. A two-stage training strategy first learns structure, then refines identity and perceptual quality using ArcFace, VGG-based, local, and region-adaptive losses. The final model reached 0.7117 ArcFace similarity and 0.1985 LPIPS on the held-out test set.

R / 03
Research · Medical AI2025

Hybrid Feature-Fusion Network with Knowledge Distillation for Tooth Disease Classification

Built a four-class DENTEX 2023 dental X-ray classifier using a ConvNeXt-Tiny + Swin-Tiny teacher with CBAM attention and a distilled ResNet34 student. The teacher reached 82.23% accuracy; the student retained 80.80% accuracy and 0.7463 macro-F1 while reducing model size. Grad-CAM was used for visual explainability.
